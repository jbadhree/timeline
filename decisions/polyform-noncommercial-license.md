## Context

Timeline is ready for its first release, and without a license nobody may legally use it. The goal is for people to use and improve it for themselves, but not to sell it. Open-source licenses, including the AGPL, all allow selling, so none of them fits.

## Consequences

Timeline is source-available rather than open source: noncommercial use, changes, and sharing are allowed, and commercial use needs separate permission. Releases include the license, and contributions are accepted under the same terms. Some contributors and package managers only accept open-source licenses, and relicensing later would need contributors' agreement.
