## Why

Timeline gives agents and teams a trustworthy record of what was decided, expected, and done, and when it was known. Teams running many agents across many repos need that record in one place, kept safely, with history and controls; that is something they will pay for, and a services practice helps them adopt it well.

## How

- [Make it easy for any agent and person to start using Timeline](agent-access.md)
- [Choose license and contributor terms that fit a paid hosted product](license-strategy.md)
- [Learn what teams will pay for, with design partners and a services practice](design-partners.md)
- [Keep every recorded event, with history and integrity, in a hosted store](hosted-record-store.md)
- [Show an organization its goals, decisions, and agent accuracy across repos](cross-repo-view.md)
- [Meet enterprise requirements for security, compliance, and data location](enterprise-readiness.md)

## Done when

- Paying teams use the hosted product, and enterprises can pass a security review.
- The free CLI stays usable on its own, with files as the source of truth.

## Why it will work

The free tool spreads through agents and developers, services teach teams to use it well and reveal what they need, and the hosted store sells what files alone cannot give: history, integrity, a cross-repo view, and enterprise controls.
